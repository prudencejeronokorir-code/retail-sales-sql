# Executive Business Insights & Strategic Recommendations  

---

### 1: Revenue Volume vs. Profit Margin Realities
* **Q16 & Q33**
* **Finding:** While **Computing** and **Mobile Phones** generate the highest aggregate sales turnover, categories like * **Accessories** deliver the highest net profit margin percentage (often exceeding 35–40%), despite contributing a lower nominal gross sales figure.
* The business currently invests significant working capital and floor space into high-ticket devices that operate on thinner margins. Conversely, high-margin impulse items are under-leveraged at the point of sale.
* Implement an active checkout cross-selling policy. When customers purchase phones or laptops, bundle essential accessories (e.g., chargers, protective cases, screen guards) at a modest 5% discount to lift basket margin without hurting unit economics.

---

### 2: Geographic Revenue Concentration
* **Q25 & Q17** 
* **Finding:** Completed sales revenue is heavily clustered in primary economic hubs like **Nairobi** and **Mombasa**, which together account for the lion's share of company turnover. Regional branches like **Machakos** and **Meru** display healthy customer registration volumes but significantly lower completed revenue per customer.
*  Capital is tied up in branch inventory and fixed retail overhead across peripheral towns where conversion into high-value sales remains sluggish.
* Reallocate expensive inventory hubs to Nairobi and Mombasa to avoid regional stockouts. For regional towns, pivot to a hub-and-spoke fulfillment model backed by targeted local M-Pesa promotions on essential everyday appliances.

---

### 3: Significant Dormant Customer Batch 
* **Q34 & Q23** 
* **Finding:** In addition to 12 registered customers who have never placed an order, over 20% of previous buyers have gone more than 90 days without a completed purchase relative to the latest dataset date (September 30, 2026).
* Customer acquisition cost (CAC) in retail electronics is substantially higher than customer retention. A customer inactive for more than 3 months is at high risk of permanently defecting to competing tech vendors.
* Set up an automated re-engagement workflow triggered on day 60 of inactivity, offering a personalized KSh 500 voucher code valid for 14 days on their next accessory or maintenance order.

---

### 4: High Value of VIP and Corporate Segments
* **Q27 & Q28** 
* **Finding:** Average customer spending in the **Corporate** and **VIP** segments is more than 3× greater than that of standard **Retail** consumers, with VIP customers showing strong repeat volume in Computing and Mobile devices.
* Corporate and VIP clients generate predictable cash flow and significantly larger order values, making them the most cost-effective accounts to service on a per-shilling basis.
* Formalize a dedicated B2B Key Accounts desk with dedicated account managers, priority stock reservations, and 30-day net billing terms to secure long-term institutional supply contracts.

---

### 5: Revenue Volatility and Month-over-Month Seasonality
* **Q38, Q40, & Q43** 
* **Finding:** Month-over-month (MoM) revenue growth exhibits pronounced cyclicality, characterized by sharp spending spikes at quarter-ends and back-to-school periods, followed by sudden double-digit drops in succeeding months.
* Unpredictable demand swings trigger stock shortages during peaks and excess carrying costs/cash-flow constraints during post-peak slumps.
* Smooth monthly cash flows by introducing structured preventative maintenance and extended warranty service contracts that bill either quarterly or semi-annually during historically low-revenue months.
